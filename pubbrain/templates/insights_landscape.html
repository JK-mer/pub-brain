Landscape

One point per summarized publication, placed by the meaning of its one-liner — neighbours read alike, whatever the vocabulary says. Size — type weight (a Report counts 8, a Comment 1); color — the cluster of the primary topic, named exactly in the tooltip. The layout is computed once and cached, so it is the same picture on every visit and a place, once learned, stays learned. New publications are slotted in beside their neighbours without moving anyone. Podcasts excluded (#50). Data: landscape.json
